MODEL CAVEATS

• FY2023 and FY2024 are n/a throughout. Gemini IPO'd in 2025; no audited annual data exists before that. This is a one-historical-column model.

• FY2026 Sales of $174.1M sits about 10% below consensus of $193.2M. The entire gap is Q3, which is marked to the July exchange and prediction tape rather than to the consensus assumption of sequential acceleration.

• Net Cash is held flat at -$252.8M across all forecast years on a funding-neutral assumption. FY2025 FCF was -$220M, so this is the weakest assumption in the model and should be replaced with a full cash-flow schedule before any liquidity conclusion is drawn.

• Market Cap is held flat at the current $513M with no price assumption, so forward EV/Sales reads as the multiple the market pays today for each forecast year's revenue.

• All Net Income and EPS figures exclude crypto mark-to-market.

• Two warehouse data-quality issues were identified and corrected: GEMI Q1 2025 adjusted EBITDA is stored as -$59,925,000,000 (a 1,000x scale error) and Q2 2025 D&A as $7,534,000,000 (same error). Neither corrupted figure is used in this model; the clean FY-level values were used instead.

• The warehouse field retail_trading_volume for Q1 2026 is tagged $6.3B but reconciles as TOTAL trading volume (institutional $5.0B plus retail ~$1.3B). Verified against Q3 2025 ($14.6B + $1.8B = $16.4B reported) and Q4 2025 ($9.9B + $1.6B = $11.5B reported). Do not double count.
